Bill The Dev has opened the beta of Slam Cards, an online version of a schoolyard game many people in Vietnam grew up with. It is free and plays in the browser at billthedevstudio.io.vn/slam-cards, on phones and computers, with nothing to install.

Each player puts a card on the table and takes turns slapping the table. The gust of the slap flips their card; landing it on a friend's card wins that card. One player makes a room and sends the link, and 2 to 4 players can join from their own browser.

The beta is focused on the feel of the slap, the rooms, and how the game holds up on weaker mobile networks. [LANGUAGES AND PRIVACY POLICY LINK]

:::person Bill (Trương Ngọc Châu) | Founder, Bill The Dev
Slam Cards is a game you start because your friends are already in the chat. A link that opens straight to the table is faster than asking everyone to install an app.
:::
