Bill The Dev, a one-man game studio from Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam, opened its website at billthedevstudio.com today. The site brings together the studio's four games, its services for clients and its news, in English and Vietnamese.

Each game has its own page with a trailer and screenshots. Slam Cards plays in the browser; the three Android games are in testing on Google Play, and their store links go live when testing ends. A services section explains the client work the studio takes on, from Unity games and VR to websites, AI assistants and shader work, and how a project runs from the first call to the handover.

Visitors can also ask Lucy, the studio's AI assistant. She answers questions about the games, the services and the founder from the studio's own notes, in the language the question is asked in.

I want to bring something unique into the world. Bill The Dev Studio is the first island I’ve landed on, and the One Piece is still out there.
:::

## The team work behind the studio

Bill The Dev is new, but its founder is not new to games. Before starting the studio, Bill spent more than five years inside other teams, working alongside artists, designers and engineers on games played by millions of people. These are some of the projects he was part of.

:::project /img/news/past/shmackle-week41.webp | Shmackle | CurlyBlue · VR on Meta Quest | https://www.meta.com/experiences/shmackle/8557045787751880/
A social VR platformer from the creator jmancurly, rated 4.7 on the Meta Quest store by about 23,000 players. Bill joined the CurlyBlue team in 2025 and contributed to the game's regular updates, including its big graphics update and new characters such as Blood Jman.
:::

:::video iPCC-0S9gXE /img/news/past/shmackle-week43.webp
Shmackle's official cinematic trailer, by jmancurly and CurlyBlue.
:::

:::project /img/news/past/applaydu.webp | Applaydu | Gameloft for brands and Ferrero · iOS and Android | https://www.kinder.com/applaydu
Kinder's edutainment app for children, made by Gameloft for brands with Ferrero. In 2023 Bill was one of the developers on the Gameloft team in Ho Chi Minh City that worked on the app.
:::

:::project /img/news/past/animal-push-royale.webp | Animal Push Royale | akaVerse, FPT IS · Mobile | https://www.facebook.com/share/p/19mGWwNz3E/
A mobile party brawler by the akaVerse team at FPT IS. It was shown at Vietnam GameVerse 2024 and reached the Top 6 of the GameHub 2024 finals. Bill was one of the team's developers.
:::

:::project /img/news/past/akaverse-nvidia-ai-summit-2024.webp | akaVerse at NVIDIA AI Summit 2024 | akaVerse, FPT IS · VR and digital twin | https://www.facebook.com/share/p/14yjJ1gCGVE/
The akaVerse team brought its VR work to NVIDIA AI Summit 2024 in Japan, where NVIDIA founder Jensen Huang stopped by the FPT booth. Bill was part of the akaVerse team behind that VR work.
:::

Each of these was a team effort, and Bill The Dev now brings that experience to its own games and to its clients.

The privacy policy of each game keeps its existing address, so nothing changes for players. More languages are planned.
