Bill The Dev has entered Holo Bastion in the Meta VR Start Developer Competition 2026. It is a mixed reality tower defense for Meta Quest 3 and 3S, and it is played with bare hands only: no controllers at any point, from the first menu to the last boss.

A holographic island lands on a real table in your room. Rifts tear open at its edge, swarms march down the roads toward the Core in the middle, and you hold them off by building turrets and casting skills with your hands. The headset finds the table on its own; if there is none, you press a palm on any flat surface and the island snaps there.

## Built for hands

Every action has its own physical move. You pinch a turret card from the tray and drop it on a pad, and dropping the same gun on itself merges it, up to tier 4. A finger gun shoots down energy drones. Skills start as orbs you pull out of the tray, then you cast them: slam a fist onto the table, push an open palm to pour fire on a road, chop with the edge of your hand, press a palm down for a Nova, or join both hands into a frame to fire a Prism beam that sweeps the lane.

Weather fronts change the island in the middle of a fight, from frost to crystal and more, and the Brood Mother arrives near the end. When you want more room, Full Dive lifts the island off the table and floats it in deep space.

## What's next

The competition's judges play the game on Meta Quest 3 and 3S over the coming weeks, and the winners are announced around 11 December 2026. In the meantime the studio is polishing the game's sound and working toward a release on the Meta Horizon Store, with more sectors and enemy families to come. The trailer is on the studio's [YouTube channel](https://youtu.be/1U1WmmL50uo).
