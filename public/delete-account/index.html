Delete your leaderboard data

Applies to Nah Blocks, Bruh Arrows and Meh Merge by Bill The Dev · Last updated: 20 October 2026

In short
  • Our games have no login. When a game shows leaderboards, it creates an anonymous player for this install on our ranking server: a random player ID, a name like Nah#4821 (which you can change) and your submitted scores.
  • You can delete all of it yourself, inside the game, at any time. It is removed from the server right away.
  • If you no longer have the game, email us and we delete it for you.

1. Delete it in the game (fastest)

  1. Open the game (Nah Blocks, Bruh Arrows or Meh Merge).
  2. Open Settings (the pause button during a game).
  3. Tap Delete ranking data, then Delete.

Your player, your name and every score you sent are deleted from our server immediately, and the game forgets its player on your phone. Your best score, coins, skins and purchases stay on your phone: they were never on our server. If you play a ranked run again later, a new anonymous player is created.

2. Ask us to delete it

If you uninstalled the game or cannot open it, email support@billthedevstudio.com with the subject Delete my leaderboard data, and tell us:

  • the game, and
  • your name on the leaderboard, including its tag (for example Nah#4821).

We delete the player and all of its scores within 30 days and reply to confirm. We never ask for a password, and you do not need to send any other personal information.

3. What is deleted, and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Player ID, leaderboard name and tagDeleted
Submitted runs and scores (all boards: weekly, all-time, daily)Deleted
Server backupsOld backups that still hold it are erased within 30 days
Data on your phone (best score, coins, skins, settings)Not on our server; stays until you clear the app's data or uninstall
PurchasesHandled by Google Play, not by us; they stay on your Google account

Tiếng Việt: xoá dữ liệu bảng xếp hạng

  • Game không có đăng nhập. Khi bật bảng xếp hạng, game tạo một người chơi ẩn danh trên server của chúng tôi: một mã ngẫu nhiên, tên dạng Nah#4821 (đổi được) và các điểm bạn đã gửi.
  • Xoá trong game: mở game → Cài đặt → Xoá dữ liệu xếp hạng → Xoá. Dữ liệu trên server bị xoá ngay. Kỷ lục, xu, skin và các món đã mua trên máy vẫn giữ nguyên.
  • Không mở được game: gửi email tới support@billthedevstudio.com, ghi tên game và tên trên bảng xếp hạng kèm mã (ví dụ Nah#4821). Chúng tôi xoá trong vòng 30 ngày và trả lời xác nhận.
